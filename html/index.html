Sentinel Technical Manual

Operational guidelines for administrators and developers

1. Server Authoritative Security

Sentinel operates on the **"Never Trust the Client"** paradigm. Client scripts can be dumped, but server-side validation is impenetrable. Always verify target locations, elapsed times, and job qualifications before executing cash or inventory payloads using the built-in exports['cts_sentinel']:VerifyAction export.

2. 3D Map Manipulation Guide

The **Interactive Map** is rendered as an isometric 3D canvas:
• **Tilt / Rotate**: Click and drag your mouse over the canvas to rotate horizontally and tilt vertically.
• **Zoom**: Scroll your mouse wheel forward/backward to zoom on specific sectors.
• **Target Details**: Hover over any player's beacon dot to view their ID, and click to open their management profile modal.

3. Native LIVE Spectating pipeline

Activating **Spectate** will automatically close the NUI focus and physically teleport and bind your game camera to the target player. When you wish to finish, pressing **`E`** or clicking "Stop Spectating" will return you to your previous coords and automatically reload this admin panel with fully refreshed statistics.
